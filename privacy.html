Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

What we collect

Circla stores account information such as your email, username and display name, plus content you choose to upload such as posts, stories, comments and messages.

How it is used

Your information is used to provide accounts, profiles, social features, messaging, notifications and story analytics. Circla does not sell your personal information.

Media

Photos and videos uploaded to Circla are stored in the connected media storage service so they can be displayed in the app.

Your control

You can stop using the service and request removal of your account data through the project owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Circla project owner through the repository or project contact channel.
